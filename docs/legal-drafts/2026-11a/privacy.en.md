## 5. The friends' quiz

A member can send a friend a link to a short quiz. If you answer it, we collect your first name, your answers (including free text), your birth date if you add it, your language and when you agreed. If you are signed in, your answers are linked to your account. You must be 18 or over to answer.

- **Who sees it:** only the member who made the quiz, in a reading prepared for them. If you add your birth date, they see your sign and life-path number, not the date.
- We do not show your answers to anyone else and do not use them to match you with anyone.
- **How long:** up to 12 months after you send them, then deleted automatically, or earlier if you or the quiz creator deletes them.
- **Deleting:** after sending, you receive a one-time link to delete your answers. If you lose it, write to the privacy address with the quiz and the first name you used.

## 6. App account

If you choose to use your current location, your phone's own location service (from Google or Apple) turns your position into a city name. Only you see your city; other members never see your city or your location. Once a match is open (both accepted), the other person sees the approximate distance between you in whole kilometres ("under 2 km" when very close), calculated from both saved locations rounded to about 1 km, never your city or your position.
- **Messages**, quizzes you create, your answers to other members' quizzes, blocks and reports.
- **Love Check:** if you check a relationship reading with someone you name, only their birth date, and their birth time and place if you add them, are sent to our server to calculate it. Their name stays on your phone. Nothing about them is stored or logged. If you search for their birth place, the text you type goes to the place search in section 11.
- **Daily Compass and daily readings:** calculated on our server from your own birth details and your time zone when you open them. We do not keep a history of them; a copy is held in our server's memory for a few hours so the screen opens faster.
- **Account emails:** verification codes, reminders to verify, password-reset codes, a notice when your password changes, and a notice when someone tries to sign up with your address. We do not send marketing emails.
- **Crash reports:** if the app hits an error, it sends us a short technical report: the error message (with any email addresses, identifiers, tokens, dates and long numbers masked), where in the code it happened, the app version, your phone's platform (Android or iOS) and the type of screen. It is sent without signing in, so it is not linked to your account, and contains no name, messages or answers. It is written as one line in our server logs, which our hosting provider keeps for at most 30 days (on our current plan, about 1 hour), and is never stored in our database.
- **Technical records:** push token and notification settings, records of what you agreed to (with version and date), and keyed one-way codes made from IP addresses or account identifiers for rate limits.

We do not ask for your sexual orientation as a separate question, and we do not send marketing emails.

**Legal bases:** the contract with you (account and readings); your consent (the friends' quiz, optional location, matching, and Google Analytics on the website, which you can withdraw at any time with **Cookie settings**, see section 15); your explicit consent (the sensitive information in section 7, for matching and compatibility readings; section 7 says which agreement covers what); our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, safety, including the record described in section 12 that stops a safety restriction being undone, and an aggregate count of website visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser); and legal obligations where they apply.

## 7. Sensitive information

Some of the information used for matching and compatibility readings is sensitive under data protection law:

- your gender, together with the genders you want to meet, which can reveal your **sexual orientation**;
- your answers about your **values and spiritual life**, which can reveal religious or philosophical beliefs;
- your answers about your **intimacy preferences**, which concern your sex life;
- interests you choose in groups such as **spirituality or psychedelics**, which can reveal beliefs or private habits;
- for this purpose we also treat your **birth details** with the same care.

**How you give your explicit consent.** Your gender and the genders you want to meet are named in the matching agreement, and agreeing to it is your explicit consent to their use for matching. **For your answers about values and spiritual life, your interests and your birth details, the app asks for your explicit consent in its own separate step**, apart from the other agreements, before matching or a compatibility result uses them. They are used now to calculate your compatibility with possible matches, and the results are shown to both members before either accepts (section 8). Both are required to take part in matching. **Your answers about intimacy have a consent of their own**, which is optional: for now only your answer about the kind of relationship you are looking for is used, to avoid suggesting matches whose answer is clearly incompatible with yours. Your other intimacy answers are kept private and are not used for any reading unless you are asked again and agree. Withdrawing that consent deletes your intimacy answers and everything worked out from them. Neither is needed for your personal readings, which you can use without them.

You can withdraw either consent at any time in the app. You then stop taking part in matching and no longer appear as anyone's possible match; your personal readings are not affected. Withdrawal does not affect earlier processing. When you withdraw either one, we delete your Quest answers and the interests you added, close your introductions and Connection Quests, and stop showing compatibility results with you. Withdrawing the consent for sensitive information also deletes your stored compatibility results with other members. Matches that are already open stay open with your conversation, and the other person keeps what they have already seen.

## 8. Matching

**Matching is not publicly available.** It is being prepared for the limited pilot. Taking part needs your agreement to matching and your explicit consent in section 7.

**How it works.** You are introduced to a possible match without names or photos. **Before either of you accepts** ("Start Quest"), both of you see the full compatibility result for the pair: the overall percentage, the result for each area, both members' answers to the Quest questions side by side, the interests you share (and pairs of related interests, which can name one of the other person's interests), how your birth charts, birth-date numbers and birth-day symbols relate, and a reading written from these results. Nobody sees your name, photos, age, city, location, birth date, time or place, or your answers to the other questionnaires at this stage. These results, especially the birth relations, can let the other person narrow down your birth date; with the age and Sun sign shown once your profiles open, sometimes to the exact day. If you both choose Start Quest, you play a Connection Quest together: the answers you give in each round are shown to the other person once the round is complete, and the app names the other person's turn as "her turn" or "his turn" from their gender. Your profiles open only when you both say yes. You can have several open matches at once.

**What an open match sees about you:** the name on your profile, your profile and cover photos, your short bio, gender and age, up to 10 of your interests, the approximate distance between you in whole kilometres, your Sun sign (only when it is certain from your birth details), your shared compatibility result (the overall percentage, the area results and a match type, when the result is verified) with a few plain-language lines on how your birth charts interact (for example "your Venus and their Saturn form a trine"), and the messages you send them. If you both turn them on in Match controls, you also see each other's answers to some of the other questionnaires, and a Dreamspell or numbers reading about the two of you; together with your age and Sun sign, such a reading can let the other person narrow down your birth date to a few days, sometimes the exact day. We never show other members your email, your full birth date, time or place, your other signs or the numbers from your readings, your planet positions, your city or your location, and we show your answers to the other questionnaires only if you both turn sharing on (your Quest answers are shown as described above).

**How possible matches are found.** Only members with a complete profile are compared: birth details (the birth time can be unknown), answers to the relationship questionnaires, matching preferences and agreements, and one photo. Between these members, we compare age and age range, gender and who each person wants to meet, the kind of connection each of you is looking for, distance if either set a limit (before a match opens distance is only a filter; after it opens, only the approximate distance in whole kilometres is shown), your answers to the Quest questions, your interests, and details calculated from birth data. The method is experimental during the pilot and may change. This is profiling. Members who have blocked each other are never matched. We store the results of comparisons between two members on our server; they are deleted when either member deletes their account.

**Compatibility.** The result a pair sees is fixed when they are introduced, so later changes do not alter the numbers. Passion & Intimacy is shown only as a general level, never as a number, and never your intimacy answers. A compatibility result uses both members' information only with both members' explicit consent (section 7); without it, there is no result. We never judge how well two people suit each other from one person's sign or number alone.

**Planned changes, not active now:**

- **Automatic acceptance.** Later you may be able to choose that a possible match is accepted on your side without your review when it meets a minimum you set; it still opens only when the other member's conditions are met too. It is switched off. We will explain it in the app and update this notice before it can be turned on, and you will always be able to choose to review each match yourself.
- **Previews.** A later version may show a heavily blurred photo preview of a possible match before it opens. This will happen only after we tell you in the app and update this notice.

If you believe an automated step affects you unfairly, write to the privacy address and a person will review it. We will carry out a data protection impact assessment before matching becomes publicly available.

Reports and their evidence are kept for **12 months from submission**, then deleted automatically, even if an account is deleted (the link to the accounts is removed; in the few cases described in section 12, the reports can be linked to a new account again). ## 15. Cookies and local storage {#cookies-local-storage}

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you allow Google Analytics, with **Accept** and **Reject** as equal choices. Until you accept, nothing is loaded from Google and no Google cookies are set; if you reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics never loads. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (names beginning with `_ga`) to tell visits apart. They last up to 2 years. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw, we delete these cookies and Google Analytics stops loading. Until you accept, any Google Analytics cookies left from an earlier visit are deleted when you open the website. Your consent is the legal basis for Google Analytics.

Vercel Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without storing anything in your browser, so it runs whatever you choose. Private quiz links and deletion links are never sent to either analytics tool.

Your browser's local storage keeps your cookie choice and what the tarot features need:

| Item | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your cookie choice | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected Google Analytics, so the banner does not ask again | 12 months, then the banner asks again; you can change it at any time |
| A random identifier | Keeps your card of the day the same all day; our server does not store it | Deleted after 365 days without use; you can clear it at any time |
| Today's card | Shows it again without a new request | Until local midnight |
| Your last draw and its question | Refreshing the page does not redraw | 30 days |

The site works without them. In the app, your phone stores sign-in tokens, unsent drafts and cached screens. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.

## 16. Security

Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, photos are delivered through signed, expiring links, and IP addresses for abuse protection are stored only as keyed one-way codes. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS), sign-in tokens last 15 minutes and sessions end after 7 days without use, new passwords are checked against known data breaches (section 11), and crash reports are masked before they are logged.
