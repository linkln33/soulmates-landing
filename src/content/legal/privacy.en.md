## 5. The friends' quiz

A member can send a friend a link to a short quiz. If you answer it, we collect your first name, your answers (including free text), your birth date if you add it, your language and when you agreed. If you are signed in, your answers are linked to your account. You must be 18 or over to answer.

- **Who sees it:** only the member who made the quiz, in a reading prepared for them. If you add your birth date, they see your sign and life-path number, not the date.
- We do not show your answers to anyone else and do not use them to match you with anyone.
- **How long:** up to 12 months after you send them, then deleted automatically, or earlier if you or the quiz creator deletes them.
- **Deleting:** after sending, you receive a one-time link to delete your answers. If you lose it, write to the privacy address with the quiz and the first name you used.

## 6. App account

- **Sign-in:** email and password (stored only as a one-way hash), or Google, Apple or an email link.
- **Birth details:** date, time (or "unknown") and place of birth, for your personal readings and to confirm you are 18 or over.
- **Profile:** name, short bio, gender identity, photos.

- **Technical records:** push token and notification settings, records of what you agreed to (with version and date), and keyed one-way codes made from IP addresses or account identifiers for rate limits.

We do not ask for your sexual orientation as a separate question, and we do not send marketing emails.

## 8. Matching

**Matching is not publicly available.** It is being prepared for the limited pilot. In the intended model, possible matches stay concealed, and a match opens only when both members review it and choose to accept.

To find possible matches we compare, between members who take part: age and age range, gender and who each person wants to meet, distance if either set a limit, answers to the app's questions, and details calculated from birth data. This is profiling. Members who have blocked each other are never matched.

We never show other members your email, your full birth date, time or place, your exact location, or your answers. The app will tell you what a possible match can see before you agree. If you believe an automated step affects you unfairly, write to the privacy address and a person will review it. We will carry out a data protection impact assessment before matching becomes publicly available.

## 9. Blocks and reports

You can block a member and report a profile or conversation in the app. A report saves what you write, a copy of the profile as you saw it, for conversations your latest messages, and for some reasons copies of the member's photos. The member is not told who reported them.

Reports and their evidence are kept for **12 months from submission**, then deleted automatically, even if an account is deleted (the link to the accounts is removed). See [Safety and reporting](/en/safety).

## 14. Browser and device storage

The website sets no cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps only what the tarot features need:

| Item | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| A random identifier | Keeps your card of the day the same all day; our server does not store it | Deleted after 365 days without use; you can clear it at any time |
| Today's card | Shows it again without a new request | Until local midnight |
| Your last draw and its question | Refreshing the page does not redraw | 30 days |

The site works without them. In the app, your phone stores sign-in tokens, unsent drafts and cached screens. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.
