## 5. The friends' quiz

A member can send a friend a link to a short quiz. If you answer it, we collect your first name, your answers (including free text), your birth date if you add it, your language and when you agreed. If you are signed in, your answers are linked to your account. You must be 18 or over to answer.

- **Who sees it:** only the member who made the quiz, in a reading prepared for them. If you add your birth date, they see your sign and life-path number, not the date.
- We do not show your answers to anyone else and do not use them to match you with anyone.
- **How long:** up to 12 months after you send them, then deleted automatically, or earlier if you or the quiz creator deletes them.
- **Deleting:** after sending, you receive a one-time link to delete your answers. If you lose it, write to the privacy address with the quiz and the first name you used.

## 8. Matching

**Matching is not publicly available.** It is being prepared for the limited pilot. Taking part needs your agreement to matching and your explicit consent in section 7.

**How it works.** Possible matches stay concealed. While a match is concealed, neither of you sees the other's name, photos, profile, city or distance, or whether the other has accepted; you see only that a possible match exists, its status and your own choice. A match opens only when both members accept it. You can have several open matches at once.

**What an open match sees about you:** the name on your profile, your profile and cover photos, your short bio, gender and age, up to 10 of your interests, your Sun and Moon signs and your life-path number, and the messages you send them. We never show other members your email, your full birth date, time or place, your city, your location or distance, or your answers to the app's questions.

**How possible matches are found.** Between members who take part, we compare age and age range, gender and who each person wants to meet, distance if either set a limit (distance is only a filter and is never shown), answers to the app's questions, and details calculated from birth data. This is profiling. Members who have blocked each other are never matched. We store the results of comparisons between two members on our server; they are deleted when either member deletes their account.

**Compatibility.** No compatibility score or percentage is shown yet. One will appear only after our pair scoring has been verified, and we will update this notice before then. A compatibility reading for an open match uses both members' information only with both members' explicit consent. We never judge how well two people suit each other from one person's sign or number alone.

**Planned changes, not active now:**

- **Automatic acceptance.** Later you may be able to choose that a possible match is accepted on your side without your review when it meets a minimum you set; it still opens only when the other member's conditions are met too. It is switched off. We will explain it in the app and update this notice before it can be turned on, and you will always be able to choose to review each match yourself.
- **Previews.** A later version may show a heavily blurred photo preview of a possible match before it opens. This will happen only after we tell you in the app and update this notice.

If you believe an automated step affects you unfairly, write to the privacy address and a person will review it. We will carry out a data protection impact assessment before matching becomes publicly available.

## 15. Cookies and local storage {#cookies-local-storage}

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you allow Google Analytics, with **Accept** and **Reject** as equal choices. Until you accept, nothing is loaded from Google and no Google cookies are set; if you reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics never loads. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (names beginning with `_ga`) to tell visits apart. They last up to 2 years. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw, we delete these cookies and Google Analytics stops loading. Until you accept, any Google Analytics cookies left from an earlier visit are deleted when you open the website. Your consent is the legal basis for Google Analytics.

Vercel Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without storing anything in your browser, so it runs whatever you choose. Private quiz links and deletion links are never sent to either analytics tool.

Your browser's local storage keeps your cookie choice and what the tarot features need:

| Item | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your cookie choice | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected Google Analytics, so the banner does not ask again | 12 months, then the banner asks again; you can change it at any time |
| A random identifier | Keeps your card of the day the same all day; our server does not store it | Deleted after 365 days without use; you can clear it at any time |
| Today's card | Shows it again without a new request | Until local midnight |
| Your last draw and its question | Refreshing the page does not redraw | 30 days |

The site works without them. In the app, your phone stores sign-in tokens, unsent drafts and cached screens. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.
